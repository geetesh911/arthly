# Fund holdings and portfolio exposure

Use official AMC monthly portfolio disclosures through the existing relay, with the same ingestion contract supported by the optional public price feed. Discover current files from official listings; never construct future workbook URLs from a guessed filename. Initial verified adapters cover PPFAS, Nippon India, HDFC and Zerodha, including their mutual funds and ETFs; [more fund houses](2026-10-23-more-fund-houses-design.md) followed. Show source failures and unsupported funds explicitly.

The relay serves a whole public disclosure manifest and proxies only allowlisted official workbook URLs. Parse workbooks in the browser data worker to avoid expensive Excel processing in Cloudflare requests. Bound response sizes, redirect destinations and concurrent downloads. Download whole supported source datasets, without sending investor holdings or scheme selections upstream. Preserve the latest successful portfolio per scheme and original disclosure date through failures and encrypted cache reloads.

Parse formatted percentages carefully: some AMCs store 0.08 with an 8% format, others store the number 8 in a percentage-points column. Read scheme name/date from inside the workbook, skip subtotals and later performance/derivative tables, retain cash and debt, and validate the weight total. The reported percentage of net assets is the weight basis; never rescale physical equities to 100%. Track derivatives separately from physical stock ownership.

Map schemes through AMFI code/ISIN and exact canonical scheme names after removing distribution-plan suffixes; ambiguous matches remain unavailable. Expand fund-of-fund positions recursively when a matched underlying fund portfolio is available, with cycle/depth protection and an explicit unresolved remainder.

Combine direct stocks and disclosed physical equity positions (current fund value multiplied by the reported weights), using security ISIN to consolidate exposure across funds/accounts. Sector and market-cap charts use constituent stock data; uncovered fund amounts remain unclassified. Underlying asset allocation preserves cash/debt/commodity portions and signed cash adjustments. Stock exposure is an estimate based on dated weights and is not derivative-adjusted risk exposure. Do not invent cost basis or stock-level gains for indirect holdings.

Add fund-level holding tables with published weight, estimated INR exposure, source/date and coverage, plus pairwise physical-stock overlap. Keep existing wrapper holdings and their gains separate. Show missing, old and retained reports and cap-size proxies clearly. Verify source parsing/allowlists, weighted arithmetic, duplicates, partial/failed refreshes, FoF recursion, member filters and desktop/phone rendering.
