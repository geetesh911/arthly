Open Arthly

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Arthly is a free, open-source portfolio tracker for Indian investors. It runs in your browser. This page says what it does with your data, wherever it's hosted. The code is public on GitHub, so you can check every word.

In short

  • Your portfolio is encrypted on your device. No server ever sees it, ours included.
  • No sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no outside scripts.
  • If you turn on cloud sync, only encrypted files go to your own Google Drive.

What stays on your device

Everything you type in or import (holdings, transactions, loans, cards, plans and the names of family members) is read and stored in your browser. It's encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key that only your passphrase, your recovery code or your device's passkey can unlock. Files you import are read in your browser, then dropped: they're never uploaded or kept.

Backup files and exports are saved wherever you choose. Backups are encrypted; Excel and JSON exports aren't, and the app warns you before saving one.

What the site sees

The site sends you the app's files and runs two small functions:

  • The price relay fetches whole public files for the app: fund prices from AMFI, stock prices and index levels from NSE and BSE, AMFI's list of company sizes and the fund houses' monthly portfolios. It always fetches whole files, so it never learns what you hold.
  • The Google token function is used only for cloud sync, as described below.

Arthly stores nothing about you on the server. Like any website, its host (Cloudflare) gets your IP address and browser details with each request, as Cloudflare's privacy policy describes.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), for the US dollar rate. It sees your IP address, never your data.
  • A price feed, only if you enter one in Settings.
  • Google, only if you turn on cloud sync.

Cloud sync with Google Drive

Cloud sync is off unless you connect Google Drive in Settings.

  • What Arthly can see: it asks only for Google's drive.file permission, so it can see and change only the files it makes itself. It can't see anything else in your Drive.
  • What it stores in your Drive: an Arthly folder holding your portfolio's encrypted files. Google, or anyone who can open your Drive, can see those files, their sizes, when they change and names that show each device's ID, but never what's in them.
  • Your email address: Arthly reads it from Google Drive to show which account is connected. It stays on that device.
  • Staying signed in: Google gives each device a token that lets it keep syncing. It's encrypted with your portfolio's key on that device. It's never synced and never put in a backup file.
  • The token function: Google needs a secret to issue those tokens, and a secret can't be kept in a web page, so the site's function adds it. Google's sign-in codes and tokens pass through it, never your data, and it keeps none of them.

Arthly uses your Google data only to sync your portfolio between your devices. It doesn't sell it, share it, show ads with it or use it to train AI models, and no person reads it. Arthly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • From a device: in Settings, choose Remove this portfolio from this device, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • From Google Drive: delete the Arthly folder in your Drive, then remove Arthly under Third-party apps & services in your Google account. That stops sync on every device. Disconnect in Settings stops it on one device.

Arthly holds nothing on its server, so there's nothing for anyone to delete there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and every change is in the project's history on GitHub.

Contact

Ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Report a security problem privately through GitHub's Report a vulnerability button on the repository.
